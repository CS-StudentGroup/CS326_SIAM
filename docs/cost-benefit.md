## 3. Return on Investment (ROI) Calculation

To evaluate the long-term viability, we project the ROI over a standard **3-year software lifecycle**.

**1. Total Costs Over 3 Years:**
* Initial Development Cost: **₱324,500**
* Operational Costs (3 years): ₱152,000 × 3 = **₱456,000**
* *Total Estimated Cost (TEC):* **₱780,500**

**2. Total Benefits Over 3 Years:**
* Annual Tangible Benefits (3 years): ₱710,000 × 3
* *Total Estimated Benefits (TEB):* **₱2,130,000**

**3. Net Benefit:**
* TEB - TEC = ₱2,130,000 - ₱780,500 = **₱1,349,500**

**4. ROI Percentage:**
* Formula: `(Net Benefit / Total Estimated Cost) × 100`
* Calculation: `(₱1,349,500 / ₱780,500) × 100`
* **ROI:** **172.90%**

**5. Payback Period:**
* How long it takes to recover the initial investment and Year 1 operational cost.
* Initial Cost + Year 1 Ops = ₱324,500 + ₱152,000 = **₱476,500**.
* Since Year 1 Benefits (₱710,000) > Year 1 Total Expenditure (₱476,500), the project breaks even in **approx. 8 months** `(₱476,500 / ₱710,000 × 12)`.
